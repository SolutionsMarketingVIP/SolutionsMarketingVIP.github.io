How Facebook Ads Work (Plain Language)

If you don't know the tech, this explains what you're buying — and why week 3 looks broken even when it's not.

The Algorithm as a New Employee

Think of Meta's system as a brilliant new hire who's never met your customers. On day one, they don't know who to show your ad to. So they start guessing.

Week 1: They try wildly different people. Some react, some don't. Notes taken.
Week 2–3: The guessing gets more expensive. Results look unstable. This is normal — they're still studying.
Week 4–6: Around 50 leads' worth of data later, they finally know your customer. Costs drop, stability improves.
Month 2–3: The algorithm graduates from student to expert. Same budget, better results.

This isn't a metaphor. This IS how delivery optimization works. We call it the Learning Phase.
