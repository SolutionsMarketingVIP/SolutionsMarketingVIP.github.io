The Algorithm as a New Employee

Think of Meta's system as a brilliant new hire who's never met your customers. On day one, they don't know who to show your ad to. So they start guessing.

Week 1They try wildly different people. Some react, some don't. Notes taken.
Week 2–3The guessing gets more expensive. Results look unstable. This is normal — they're still studying.
Week 4–6Around 50 leads' worth of data later, they finally know your customer. Costs drop, stability improves.
Month 2–3The algorithm graduates from student to expert. Same budget, better results.

L'algorithme comme nouvel employé

Semaine 1Il essaie des gens très variés. Certains réagissent, d'autres non. Notes prises.
