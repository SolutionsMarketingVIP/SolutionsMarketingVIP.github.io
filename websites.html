What We Promise (and What We Don't)

We believe in honesty over hype. Here is exactly what you get from us:

  • Clean, fast, hand-written code that loads quickly on any connection
  • A website that respects your visitors: no trackers, no data collection, no third-party scripts phoning home
  • Technical SEO best practices applied to every page we build
  • Fixed pricing agreed before work starts
  • Responsive support after launch

What we cannot promise: specific search rankings on Google or Bing. No honest provider can. Search engines decide rankings based on hundreds of factors, and anyone guaranteeing you a number one position is not telling the truth. What we guarantee is professional work that gives your site its best possible chance to rank.
